Scholarship Schemes

Post-Matric Scholarship for Students with Disabilities

दिव्यांग छात्रों के लिए मैट्रिकोत्तर छात्रवृत्ति

For students with 40% or more disability studying in class XI and above. ₹ 1200/month for hostellers, ₹ 550/month for day scholars, plus fee reimbursement.

Apply for PwD scholarship →

Pre-Matric Scholarship (SC / ST / OBC)

प्री-मैट्रिक छात्रवृत्ति

For students of classes IX and X from SC, ST and OBC categories. Covers admission fee, tuition and a maintenance allowance of ₹ 500/month.

Apply for Pre-Matric scholarship →

Merit-cum-Means Scholarship

मेरिट-कम-मीन्स छात्रवृत्ति

For professional and technical courses at undergraduate and postgraduate level. Full tuition reimbursement up to ₹ 20,000/year.

Apply for Merit-cum-Means scholarship →

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship

राष्ट्रीय साधन-सह-मेधा छात्रवृत्ति

For meritorious students of economically weaker sections in class IX onwards — ₹ 12,000/year till class XII.

Apply Online

Students who cannot type can apply by voice: open the ଅ assistant and tap କହି ଲେଖ · Speak to fill — it asks for each answer aloud and never submits on its own. Say your state's name for the state box — "ଓଡ଼ିଶା" — and the scheme's name for the scheme box.

Applicant Instructions

It is hereby notified that applicants shall register on the portal with a valid mobile number and shall furnish the Aadhaar number or the enrolment ID thereof at the time of registration. Applications lacking the requisite institution verification shall be summarily rejected, in accordance with the stipulated guidelines.

Applicants belonging to the PwD category shall furnish the disability certificate issued by the competent authority, viz. the notified medical board, and are exempted from payment of the application processing fee w.e.f. 15.07.2026.
