HSC Examination 2027

The High School Certificate (HSC) examination, also known as Madhyama, is the Class 10 public examination conducted by the Board of Secondary Education. The 2027 cycle is scheduled from 4 February to 22 February 2027, and the pre-board cycle for special-school candidates will be held 21–27 January 2027.

Accessible Examinations

BSE Odisha is committed to the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (Section 17 — "Adult Education" and Section 31 — "Free Education"), and aligns its practice with the CBSE circular on PwD candidates (2023). Candidates registered through their school or centre are entitled to the following arrangements:

Additional time

+50% writing time (additional 30 min for a 3-hour paper, additional 60 min for a 4-hour paper).

Scribe / helper

A student from a lower class, pre-approved by the centre superintendent.

Braille / large print

Question papers in contracted UEB Braille and 22-point large print, on request.

Calculator / spell-checker

Simple calculator and spell-checker permitted for candidates with dyscalculia / dyslexia.

Ground-floor seating

Candidates with mobility impairment are seated in rooms that don't require climbing stairs.

AaaS integration (new)

This website now reads every page aloud in Odia, accepts spoken input for forms, and automatically flags WCAG 2.1 AA issues.
