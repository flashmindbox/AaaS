Book an OPD Appointment

Patients who cannot type can book by voice: open the ଅ assistant and tap କହି ଲେଖ · Speak to fill — it asks for each answer aloud, in Odia, and never submits on its own. Say the department's name for the department box — "କାର୍ଡିଓଲୋଜି", "ନେଫ୍ରୋଲୋଜି" — exactly as you would say it.

Patient Instructions

It is hereby notified that OPD registration counters shall function w.e.f. 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM on all working days, and the registration fee of Rs. 10 shall be remitted at the counter; patients aged 65 years and above and persons with disabilities are exempted from payment of the said fee and shall be attended on priority.

Patients availing speciality OPD services shall furnish the requisite referral slip, if any, at the time of registration, failing which consultation shall be provided subject to availability of slots, in accordance with the stipulated guidelines.
