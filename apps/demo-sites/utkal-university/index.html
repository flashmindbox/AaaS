Welcome to Utkal University

Established on 27 November 1943, Utkal University is Odisha's first university and India's 17th oldest. The Vani Vihar campus today houses 27 postgraduate departments, 167 affiliated colleges, and 2.5 lakh students across Odisha.

The university is committed to providing accessible education for every student — screen-reader-tested online portals, audio CAPTCHA, and additional time for students with disabilities.

Admissions 2026–27

Applications for postgraduate programmes are open on the Samarth portal. Special seats are reserved under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, and the entire application process is accessible — screen-reader tested, audio CAPTCHA available, and an additional 50% of exam time granted on request.

Research and Centres of Excellence

Utkal University hosts five UGC-recognised centres of excellence, including the Ambedkar Centre for Social Justice, the Heritage Studies Centre dedicated to Odia palm-leaf manuscripts, and the Disability Studies Centre established in 2024.

Over 800 research scholars are awarded doctoral degrees each year. Accessibility and social impact are central to every research programme.

Accessibility Cell

Under the RPwD Act 2016, the Utkal University Accessibility Cell provides the following services to enrolled students and applicants:

  • Scribe and additional time — on registration, +50% writing time across all examinations.
  • Accessible study material — lecture notes in large print, Braille, and screen-reader-friendly EPUB formats on request.
  • Assistive-technology lab — JAWS, NVDA, Kurzweil, Daisy Player, and sign-language video booths at the Central Library.
  • Sign-language interpretation — book by email at least 3 working days in advance.
  • Campus mobility — tactile pathways, audible lifts, and reserved accessible parking at all academic buildings.
