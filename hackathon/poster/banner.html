Six tools behind one button

1
ପଢ଼ି ଶୁଣାଅ
Read aloud

Reads the page in a natural Odia voice, highlighting each line. Says numbers correctly.

2
ଅନୁବାଦ
Translate

The whole page turns into Odia in place. Dates and amounts stay exact. One tap undoes.

3
ସହଜ ପଢ଼ା
Easy Read

Officialese becomes simple words: “furnish the requisite documents” → “give the papers”.

4
ଦଲିଲ ପଢ଼
Read document

Reads scanned notices and PDFs, shows them in Odia beside the original, and reads them aloud.

5
କହି ଲେଖ
Speak to fill

Asks each form question aloud in Odia and writes your answer. Never presses Submit for you.

6
କହି ଚଲାଅ
Voice command

Say where to go, like “ବିଜ୍ଞପ୍ତି”, and it opens the link. When unsure, it shows choices.
